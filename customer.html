Explore GEMINILE STUDY HUB

1. Choose your space

Check available spaces for your date, start time, and end time. Only spaces open for that entire window can be selected.

2. Reserve your time

Pick a space that fits your group and submit your reservation. We email you when your request is received and again when staff confirms it.

3. Check in and study

When you arrive, staff can check you in and help you get settled in your reserved space.

4. Café and orders

Browse café items and ask the front desk to place an order during your visit.

5. Billing and payments

Staff can provide your bill and record or verify payments for your visit and orders.

6. Updates and help

Your reservation status appears below. Check your email for reservation updates or contact the front desk if you need help.
